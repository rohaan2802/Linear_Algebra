## Course Overview

Linear Algebra (MT1004) is the core mathematics course of the third semester of BS Computer Science at FAST-NUCES. In Fall 2024 it ran across several sections, so the slides, homeworks and solutions came from different instructors – Muhammad Ali (sections B, C and D), M. Usman Rashid (BCS A, E, F and G) and Sara Aziz – and reached me through Google Classroom, WhatsApp and email. This repository keeps all of it together in the folders I used while studying.

The course follows *Linear Algebra and Its Applications* (4th edition) by David C. Lay.

## Topics Covered

| Unit | Topics |
|---|---|
| **1. Linear systems** | Systems of linear equations, row reduction, echelon and reduced echelon forms, vector and matrix equations, span, linear independence |
| **2. Matrices and transformations** | Matrix algebra, inverses, elementary matrices, determinants and their properties, linear transformations, standard matrix, kernel and range, one-to-one and onto, inverse transformations |
| **3. Vector spaces** | Subspaces, column and null space, basis and dimension, fundamental subspaces, coordinate systems, change of basis, geometry of vector spaces |
| **4. Eigenvalues** | Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, the characteristic polynomial, diagonalization, linear recurrences, difference and differential equations, stability and the trace-determinant plane |
| **5. Orthogonality** | Inner products, length and distance, orthogonal sets, projections, Gram-Schmidt, QR decomposition, least squares, curve fitting and regression |
| **6. Symmetric matrices and SVD** | Orthogonal diagonalization, positive definite matrices, quadratic forms, constrained optimization, the Rayleigh quotient, square root of a matrix, singular value decomposition, PCA, tensors |
